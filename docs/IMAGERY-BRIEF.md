## The four doors: what to license on Adobe Stock

Current door stills are serviceable but generic. Target: one distinctive,
inland, human image per door, consistent warm grade, no AI-looking interiors,
vertical-friendly crop (the doors render 3:4.1). Photos first; video-in-door is
possible later (the mockup already supports it) but costs bandwidth.

Search phrases (Adobe Stock, filter: Photos, orientation Vertical where offered):

1. **Buying or selling** — the life moment, inland setting:
   - "family moving boxes into new home craftsman porch golden hour"
   - "young couple front porch new house keys evening california"
   - Avoid: agent-with-clipboard cliches, staged handshake shots.
2. **Real estate agents** — the professional, not the suit cliche:
   - "realtor showing home warm interior natural light candid"
   - "real estate agent open house conversation homeowners californian bungalow"
   - Avoid: sold-sign poses, blazer-crossed-arms stock look.
3. **Lenders & loan officers** — precision and calm:
   - "architectural detail suburban rooflines aerial pattern golden hour"
   - "modern home exterior clean lines late afternoon shadows"
   - This door reads best abstract: rooflines/geometry over people.
4. **Investors & specialty** — scale and horizon, inland:
   - "san gabriel valley aerial neighborhood mountains background dusk"
   - "orange county suburban sprawl aerial foothills evening"

Budget: 4 hero door images ≈ 4-8 credits (of 111). License 2 candidates per
door only if the finalist is unclear; otherwise 1 each.
